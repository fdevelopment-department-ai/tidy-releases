01 — What it does

Three quiet things, all day long.

01

It watches.

Tidy lives in your tray. It silently monitors your Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded, nothing leaves your machine.

02

It learns.

Every time you move a file by hand, Tidy remembers. After six examples across two destinations, a personal model trains in the background.

03

It suggests.

When a new file lands, Tidy quietly proposes where it probably belongs — only if it's confident enough. You stay in charge.

02 — How it works

A model of your own habits.

01

Install & run

One binary, no setup. Tidy appears in the tray and starts watching your Downloads folder.

02

Move files manually

Keep sorting files the way you always do. Tidy records each (file → destination) pair locally.

03

It trains itself

Once enough examples are in, a scikit-learn classifier trains in the background. Char n-grams on the filename, plus extension, size and type.

04

Quiet suggestions

When confidence passes 65 %, Tidy nudges you with a notification. No auto-move. No surprises.

Your habits stay yours.

Tidy doesn't read the contents of your files. It looks at the name, the extension, the size, and a magic-byte sniff. That's it.

The trained model lives on your disk, in your user data folder. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting that folder erases everything Tidy has learned.
